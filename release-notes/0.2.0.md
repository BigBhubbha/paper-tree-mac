Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.0

- The flask now stays inside the menu bar. It is sized to the menu bar height, never hangs onto your tabs, and drags only sideways. Let go over the notch and it settles beside it.
- A new first-launch page: a taped specimen sheet where the flask introduces itself, explains why it needs Accessibility, and gets stamped the moment you switch it on.
- Built-in updates. Paper Tree checks once a day, and you can check any time from the menu bar flask with "Check for Updates...".

Coming from 0.1.0? Install this one from the DMG. Updates after this arrive on their own. After an update, if highlights stop working, switch Paper Tree off and on again in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
