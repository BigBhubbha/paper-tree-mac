Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.2

- Meet the island. The flask no longer sits in the menu bar. Instead, double-tap the right Option key and a black island grows out of the notch, like the Dynamic Island on iPhone, with Research mode, Settings, Check for Updates, and Quit inside. Double-tap again, press Esc, or click anywhere else to close it. On Macs without a notch it opens from the top center of the screen.
- Triple-tap the right Option key to turn Research mode on or off from anywhere. The island flashes "Research mode on" or "off" so you know it worked.
- Highlight and it explains. With Research mode on, just highlight a word or sentence and the explanation card opens a moment after you let go. No extra click.
- A bigger, cleaner explanation card. It is wider, grows to fit the whole answer, and scrolls if an answer runs long, so text never gets cut off. The highlighted term sits at the top like a specimen label, with Plain, Simpler, and More detail tabs and a close button.
- Shorter answers. A plain explanation is now two short sentences, Simpler is a single quick line, and More detail stays under about 90 words.
- Paper Tree is now a regular Mac app. It has its own Dock icon and shows up when you press Cmd+Tab. Click the Dock icon to open Settings. The small flask in the menu bar is still there too, with the same menu.
- A Paper Tree menu at the top of the screen when the app is in front, with About, Check for Updates, Settings, and Quit.
- Check for Updates is now in the island and at the bottom of Settings, so you never have to hunt for it.
- No more re-approving Accessibility after updates. Paper Tree is now signed with its own permanent signature, so macOS remembers your permission from one version to the next.

One last time: because this update changes how Paper Tree is signed, macOS will ask you to approve Accessibility again after installing it. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and switch Paper Tree off and back on (or remove it with the minus button and add it again). After that, future updates keep the permission.
