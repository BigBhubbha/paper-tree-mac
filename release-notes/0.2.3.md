Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.3

Faster answers
- Explanations come back much faster. In our tests a typical highlight went from about 19 seconds (and some timeouts) to about 1 to 4 seconds, and a highlight you have already explained comes back in under half a second.
- Words appear as they are written. The answer fills in on the card while it arrives, and the glossary follows when it is done.
- Switching between Plain, Simpler, and More detail for the same highlight is instant once you have seen each one.

A new explanation card
- A clean note card in the same style as the one on the Paper Tree website: the sprout at the top, the version you asked for in the corner, and a short answer in easy-to-read type.
- A mini glossary. Under the answer, up to four hard words from your highlight, each with a short plain meaning.
- Nothing gets cut off anymore. The card sizes itself to fit the answer. Only a very long answer, taller than most of your screen, scrolls inside the card.

A new setup
- Setup is now five entries in a field journal. First, a few specimen tags about you: your year in school, your science background, the fields you read most, and how long you like your notes. Circle an answer in pencil.
- Explanations now fit the reader. Your answers go along with each highlight, so a 9th grader gets everyday words and a grad student gets the real terms without the basics.
- A practice run. Double-tap the right Option key and the island opens, triple-tap and Research mode turns on, then highlight a phrase in a sample passage and a real card appears. Each step stamps itself as you do it, and you can skip it.
- For now, while Paper Tree is being tested, setup opens again after every update with your earlier answers already filled in. The Accessibility page only shows if permission is actually missing.

A new Settings
- Settings is now the instrument drawer. Your answers sit on a herbarium label; click one to change it.
- A slip in the corner counts the explanation cards this Mac has opened.
- The switches are specimen tags: circle on or off. "Copy fallback" is now called "Grab stubborn text", with a plainer description.
- The right Option shortcut is drawn as a little keyboard figure. Click the marked key to see a double tap.
- The server address and app token are tucked under "For tinkerers" at the bottom, since the explainer is built in.

Accessibility stays on after this update.
