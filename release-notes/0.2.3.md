Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.3

- A new explanation card, in the same style as the one on the Paper Tree website. It is a clean note card with the Paper Tree sprout at the top, the version you asked for (Plain, Simpler, or More detail) in the corner, and a short answer in easy-to-read type.
- A mini glossary. Under the answer, a thin line and up to four hard words from your highlight, each with a short plain meaning.
- Nothing gets cut off anymore. The card measures the answer and sizes itself to fit it exactly. Only a very long answer, taller than most of your screen, scrolls inside the card.
- The big yellow title is gone. What you highlighted now shows as one small gray line under the header.
- Plain, Simpler, More detail, Copy, and close are still there, just quieter.
