Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.4

Your trees live in the island
- Double-tap the right Option key and the island opens onto your grove: every tree you have started, drawn as a herbarium sheet. Trees stay large however many you have. With more than three they sit in two rows that scroll sideways, and the New tree card always stays in view.
- Click a tree to open its page: the whole plant on the left, its notes and papers on the right. Click a paper's title to open it.
- Triple-tap still turns Research mode on and off.

Add a paper with a single tap
- Highlight a paper's title anywhere and tap the right Option key once. Paper Tree looks the title up, shows you what it found, and asks which tree it belongs on, or lets you start a new tree.
- When a title could be more than one paper, it asks "Is this it?" and shows the top three, with authors, venue, year, and how often each one is cited. If none is right, add it exactly as you highlighted it.

Trees that grow from what you read
- A new tree starts as a sapling and grows with every paper you add.
- Each tree takes a species from its topics: oak, willow, conifer, venus flytrap, alien tree, or coral tree. They now look clearly different even when small: the conifer is a dark spruce spire, the flytrap has lime leaves with red-mouthed traps, and the coral tree is a salmon fan of branches.

Front pages at the branch tips
- Each paper hangs from its branch as a tiny copy of its real first page. Paper Tree now checks more places for a free copy, including arXiv and open-access sources.
- If no free copy exists, you see a hand-drawn title page instead.

Suggestions in pencil
- Every tree sketches a few pencil branches: related papers you might want next. Keep one and it turns to ink, or prune it.
- Suggestions now keep working when one paper database is busy, by asking another.

Fix a wrong match
- If a paper landed as the wrong one, hover over it on the tree's page and choose "Wrong paper? Change the match", or right-click it. Pick the right one and it keeps its place on the tree.
- Papers you added as highlighted can be matched later with "Find a match".

For now, while Paper Tree is being tested, setup still opens again after an update with your earlier answers filled in.
