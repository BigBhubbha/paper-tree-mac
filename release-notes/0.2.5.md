Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.5

A new shortcut
- Tap the right Option key once to open the island onto your trees. Tap it again, or press Esc, to put it away.
- If you have a paper's title highlighted when you tap, it shows at the top of the island with an "Add this paper" button. Choose it and Paper Tree finds the paper and asks which tree it belongs on, just as before.
- Double-tap the right Option key to turn Research mode on or off. The triple tap is gone.
- Single taps respond faster now.
- Setup, Settings and the keyboard drawing all show the new keys.

Painted trees
- Every tree is painted fresh: tapered wood with bark and a lit side, and soft watercolor leaves laid in light and shadow, the way botanical plates are colored by hand.
- Oaks have full round crowns, willows trail long leafy strands, conifers stack in tiers, flytraps open and close their traps, alien trees carry glowing seed pods, and coral trees spread thin red branches over a pink fan.
- Trees move gently in the wind. Thin twigs sway more than the trunk.

Watch a tree grow
- When you add a paper, its branch grows out, new leaves unfold, and the paper's front page settles onto the tip.
- Pencil branches, the suggestions, draw themselves on after that.

Smoother everywhere
- The island springs open and closed, and its contents fade in without jumping.
- Explanation cards write themselves in, then the glossary appears.
- Pages in setup turn, the grove zooms into a tree's page, and buttons and links respond when you hover and press.
- If Reduce Motion is on in your Mac's settings, all of this becomes simple fades and the trees hold still.

For now, while Paper Tree is being tested, setup still opens again after an update with your earlier answers filled in.
