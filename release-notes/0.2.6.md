Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.6

Room to breathe
- The grove and every tree have much more space. Papers sit apart from each other, branches spread out, and a tree fills most of its page.
- Scroll to move around a big tree, and zoom in or out to see more or less of it.

Read inside the island
- Open a paper right in the island and read it without leaving your trees.
- Highlight, underline, add comments in the margin, and keep notes on the paper. They are saved with the paper and come back when you open it again.

Leaves you can tell apart
- Leaves are drawn one by one, with clear gaps between them, instead of a solid mass of color.

As many trees as you like
- There is no limit on how many trees you plant, and a big tree keeps up to two suggestions for every paper on it.

Plants that match what you read
- Each tree grows as one of 13 plants chosen from its papers. Animal behavior grows a flytrap, the ocean grows kelp, the brain grows dendrites, astronomy grows an alien tree, computing grows a crystal tree, medicine grows an herb, and so on. There are also oaks, willows, conifers, coral, cactus, ferns and mushrooms.
- A small label on each tree says what it was grown from.
- As you add papers on a new subject, the plant changes gradually instead of jumping.

A tree designer
- Paper Tree now designs each tree for its papers: the crown (round, columnar, vase, spreading, weeping or windswept), the trunk (single, forked, several stems, leaning or gnarled), how the branches reach, the size and spacing of the leaves, the colors, and an accent such as blossoms, berries, fruit, seed pods or softly glowing tips.
- No two trees come out the same, even when they hold similar papers.
- It only asks when a tree is planted or its subject shifts. Trees from earlier versions keep their look until you add to them.

A new tutorial
- Setup is now 11 short pages, one step at a time, with a sprig along the bottom that grows a leaf for each step.
- You try each part for real: opening the island, highlighting to get an explanation, adding a paper, and turning on Research mode.
- The flask buddy acts out every step. It waves, juggles your subjects, knocks on the permission lock, presses the Option key, peers at your highlight, carries a paper to a sapling, flips the Research switch and celebrates at the end.
- If Reduce Motion is on, the buddy holds still.

For now, while Paper Tree is being tested, setup still opens again after an update with your earlier answers filled in.
