Paper Tree for Mac 0.2.7

Cooler and quieter when idle
- Trees and the flask buddy stop redrawing when the island is closed, or when you switch away so the island is no longer the front window. They also pause if Reduce Motion is on.
- The buddy keeps a gentle idle bob at a few frames a second, and only speeds up when it is thinking or has just noticed a highlight.
- Research mode only listens for mouse highlights while it is on. Turning it off removes those listeners. Tapping right Option still works either way.
- The setup tutorial no longer opens again after every update. It only runs the first time, or when you choose Redo setup.
